**Droidream** is a 2D Action Roguelike developed in Godot Engine with a great emphasis on its unique combat mechanics inspired by early 2000s turn-based RPGs. The game takes heavy inspiration from the “Paper Mario”, “Undertale” and “WarioWare” game series and tries to blend their different gameplay styles into a fun and new twist on the turn based RPG genre. It will try to have a great emphasis on its combat mechanics, roguelike elements and minigames.

**What makes it different?** The combat tries to capture the simplicity and strategies used in “Paper Mario” and “Mario & Luigi” games, where the player will be able to “attack” and block different enemy attack patterns by reacting to on-screen action, including adapting to different foes’ minigame centered sequences in the style of “WarioWare” microgames, which have to be completed in order to win battles more easily. These sequences will be quick 5-10 second minigames where the player has to react to various different challenges (i.e. a quick round of a “Space Shooters” clone, a “Geometry Dash” styled parkour game, a panning flashlight hide-and-seek game, or a rhythm based rope jumping game just to name a few) to progress the fight. Losing these minigames should not feel that punishing, but affect the pace of the battle in a way that the player would prefer winning them. This style of gameplay would make standard turn-based battles more interesting, as players would have to keep more alert and be eager to face new foes with new attack patterns and minigames.
